Introduction

On August 3, 2026, at approximately 03:00 local time (Africa/Cairo; 2026-08-03 00:00 UTC), a magnitude 5 earthquake, with a reported depth of 10 km, occurred 39 km NNE of Suez, Egypt. The USGS epicenter was at 30.3186° latitude and 32.6422° longitude.

Building Damage

Initial official and humanitarian reports indicated no immediate property damage from the earthquake [1][3][7]. However, a more specific report stated that a balcony collapsed in Suez and an old residential building in Cairo’s Rod El-Farag district sustained partial damage; three families were evacuated from that building as a precaution [9]. Buildings were reported shaking across Cairo, while residents in Greater Cairo, the Suez Canal region, and several Egyptian governorates experienced sustained shaking and, in some locations, left buildings as a precaution [2][8]. The Egyptian Red Crescent advised residents to avoid old, dilapidated, or structurally weakened buildings—particularly those with visible cracks or other signs of structural damage—until assessments were completed [2][5][6]. Such older buildings were reported as common in Cairo’s densely populated working-class neighborhoods [2][4].

Infrastructure Impact

NRIAG located the epicenter approximately 38–41 km (24–25 miles) north-northeast of Suez city, near the Great Bitter Lakes stretch of the Suez Canal [10]. The Suez Canal Authority reported no disruption to maritime traffic [11].

Resilience and Recovery

During the first hours, officials reported no casualties or property damage [12]. A subsequent Health Ministry update reported one injury nationwide [13]. Separately, a woman's family reported that she died after collapsing while shielding her children during the tremor; the same report said the Health Ministry recorded one injury and no official fatalities [14]. The family-reported death and the official fatality count are therefore presented as distinct, unresolved attribution levels. Health Minister Khaled Abdel-Ghaffar ordered nationwide emergency-response activation and maximum ambulance preparedness; authorities also evacuated three families from a damaged residential building in Rod al-Farag [15]. The ministry convened its central crisis and emergency operations room [16]. The Egyptian Red Crescent received a report of a balcony collapse in Suez with no associated casualties [14].
